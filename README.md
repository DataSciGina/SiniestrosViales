# Siniestros Viales en CABA

Data analysis project focused on understanding fatal road traffic accidents in the City of Buenos Aires using official public datasets.

---

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes road traffic accidents with fatalities in Buenos Aires City, aiming to identify patterns and risk factors that can support prevention strategies and public policy decisions.

The analysis combines multiple datasets related to accidents, victims, time, location, and road characteristics.

## 🧠 Methodology

- Data cleaning and integration from multiple sources.
- Exploratory data analysis to identify trends and anomalies.
- Frequency and percentage analysis of categorical variables.
- Visualization of temporal, demographic, and spatial patterns.
- Insight generation to support decision-making.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

- Most fatal accidents occur during early morning hours.
- Avenues and major roads concentrate the highest number of incidents.
- Motorcyclists and pedestrians are the most affected groups.
- Victims are predominantly males aged between 20 and 40.
- Accidents are highly concentrated in specific areas and road types.
